## oh-my-shells Code of Conduct

This project is meant to be for learning, building, and having fun with programming. To keep things running nicely, here’s what we expect from everyone:

### Be Cool to Each Other

Treat people with respect. No harassment, slurs, personal attacks, or intentionally making others uncomfortable. Remember there's an actual person behind the screen.

### Keep It Helpful

Share feedback in a constructive way. Celebrate wins, point out issues kindly, and keep discussions focused on improving the project.

### Stay Inclusive

Everyone’s welcome here regardless of background, experience level, or identity. Make the space easy for newcomers to join in.

### No Spam or Chaos

Don’t derail conversations, flood issues/PRs, or introduce content meant to provoke or disrupt.

### Handle Conflicts Calmly

If something feels off, bring it up respectfully. If a maintainer steps in to mediate or make a call, please respect that decision.

### Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, lock, or remove comments, issues, or pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct. Repeated problems may lead to being blocked from the repo.

---

Thanks for helping keep this project a welcoming place!
